# Licensing, provenance and affiliation

## Original work

The original Python bridge, documentation, synthetic fixture and original
illustrations are offered under the [MIT license](LICENSE). Contributions were
developed with AI assistance and checked by executable tests. The demo's symbols,
packages and net topology are newly authored test geometry; they are not copied,
anonymized or simplified versions of a research board.

No third-party board, EAGLE library, KiCad binary or font is bundled.
Historical development involved other boards, but those boards and their results
are not evidence shipped with this release. Reproduce the included example instead.
